Institutional Framework

Vision & Mission

Grounded in the Sendai Framework and RA 10121, committing to a resilient, data-driven disaster management standard for Barangay Linao.

Vision Statement

"By 2030, Barangay Linao shall possess a digital, data-driven, and disaster-resilient management system capable of supporting efficient preparedness, response, recovery, and rehabilitation operations across its seventeen (17) puroks."

Mission Statement

"To strengthen disaster preparedness and emergency response in Barangay Linao through a GIS-integrated digital platform — improving incident tracking, resource allocation, and evacuation center facility inspection in compliance with RA 10121 and RA 10173."
